HOW TO PREPARE

  1. use 18 grams of fresh coffee grounds to yield 36 grams of liquid espresso in about 25 to 30 seconds
  2. equal parts espresso, steamed milk, and rich microfoam (traditionally layered or integrated)
  3. combine one part espresso with two to three parts steamed milk, topped with a thin layer of microfoam
  4. sift 1 to 2 grams (about 1 teaspoon) of matcha powder into a bowl, add warm water heated to 75°C–80°C (170°F–176°F), and whisk rapidly in a "W" or "M" motion until a fine foam forms
